If you have just moved to Italy, this Amazon trial is worth ten minutes of your time.

Students with a university email, and anyone aged 18 to 24, can use Amazon Prime free for 90 days. You just sign up on [this page](https://www.amazon.it/amazonprime?primeCampaignId=studentWlpPrimeRedir&language=en).

![The Amazon Prime student signup page](/assets/posts/italy-amazon-prime-student/prime-signup.png "Free for 90 days for students and 18 to 24 year olds, then €2.49 a month, half the standard price")

## Why it is worth doing on day one

Amazon is one of the largest shopping and delivery platforms in Italy. I ordered a pile of small electronics right after landing, and with Prime most of it turned up the next day.

![An Amazon order delivered the next day](/assets/posts/italy-amazon-prime-student/prime-delivery.png "Ordered 31 August, delivered 1 September. Chargers, an ethernet cable and bathroom bits in one go (order number redacted)")

If your dorm room is missing the basics, sheets and pillows and so on, order them before your move-in date. That way you are not spending your first days hunting for shops in a city you do not know yet.

## What else comes with it

Prime bundles in some other memberships, Deliveroo among them, which is more useful than it sounds when you have not worked out where to buy food yet.

![A Deliveroo order with free delivery](/assets/posts/italy-amazon-prime-student/prime-benefits.png "Delivery fee shows as Free on Deliveroo (address and card digits redacted)")
