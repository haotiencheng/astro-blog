This is a write-up of the admin a student moving from Taiwan to Italy has to get through after landing, in the order that actually worked. Hopefully it saves someone else a few wasted trips.

My girlfriend and I wanted to travel before term started, so clearing the paperwork was the first priority, and we got the important parts done in three days. Being able to relax on the trip afterwards was worth it.

## The timeline

| Date | What happened |
|---|---|
| 21 Aug | Booked the tax code appointment from Taiwan over a VPN |
| 24 Aug | Landed in Italy |
| 25 Aug | Tax code, iliad SIM, picked up the residence permit kit at the big post office by the Duomo |
| 26 Aug, midday | Went back to the big post office and were turned away |
| 26 Aug, 13:00 | Filed the kit at another branch right before it closed |
| 27 Aug | Train to Lugano |
| 2 Sep | Bought the ATM travel pass |

## The order I would recommend

Having been through it once, this is the sequence I would use:

1. **Fix your flight dates**
2. **Book the tax code appointment 7 to 14 days ahead, over a VPN** (Italian government sites block foreign IPs)
3. **Get the SIM right after the tax code appointment**, since you will be out anyway
4. **Buy the ATM travel pass**
5. **Pick up and file the residence permit application**

Done in that order, you clear the whole thing quickly and cheaply (the monthly pass being the cheap way to move around while doing it).

![The order of the four jobs](/assets/posts/italy-arrival-checklist/arrival-flow-en.png "The whole sequence on one page: the tax code gates the SIM, the pass and the permit")

The logic is simple: **the tax code gates the other three**. The carrier wants it, the travel pass wants it, and the permit form has a field for it. So book that appointment as early as you can and queue everything else behind it.
