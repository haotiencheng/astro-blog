The first thing to sort out after landing in Italy is the tax code (Codice Fiscale). Almost everything downstream needs it: residence permit, phone contract, transit pass, and so on.

My advice is to **book it the moment you land** — and to set up an Italian VPN before you leave, so you can reserve a slot in advance.

## Why you need a VPN first

Italian government sites usually block foreign IPs, so opening the booking page from home often gets you nowhere. With a VPN ready you can lock in an appointment before departure instead of burning days once you arrive.

## Booking online

The booking site: [Prenota un appuntamento — Agenzia delle Entrate](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/prenota-un-appuntamento)

**Do not switch the site to English.** The flow seems to break when you do; keeping everything in Italian is the safer path.

### 1. Pick the service

Under "Di cosa hai bisogno?" choose **Assistenza fiscale** (tax matters). Under "Per quale argomento?" choose **Identificativi fiscali, abilitazioni, consegna documenti e istanze** (tax identification).

![Choosing Assistenza fiscale and Identificativi fiscali](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-01.png "Step 1 — service: <em>Assistenza fiscale</em>; topic: <em>Identificativi fiscali</em>")

### 2. Book an upcoming slot

Take the left option, **Appuntamento per i prossimi giorni**. The Webticket on the right is a same-day queue ticket, which is not what you want here.

![Choosing Appuntamento per i prossimi giorni](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-02.png "Step 2 — take <em>Appuntamento per i prossimi giorni</em> on the left")

### 3. For yourself, first application

Choose **per te stesso** (for yourself), tick the first-time application option, leave the tax code field empty, and accept the privacy notice.

![Selecting a first-time application for yourself](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-03.png "Step 3 — for yourself, first-time application, tax code field left empty")

### 4. Fill in your details

`Cognome` is your surname, `Nome` your given name. At least one contact field is required — use email, since the confirmation lands there.

![Filling in name and contact details](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-04.png "Step 4 — <em>Cognome</em> is surname, <em>Nome</em> is given name; email is the contact to use")

### 5. Enter your starting city

Type the city you are travelling from and the system suggests nearby offices.

![Entering the starting city](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-05.png "Step 5 — enter the city you travel from; nearby offices are suggested")

### 6. Choose an office and date

You get a list of nearby Ufficio Territoriale branches with their earliest available dates. Pick one that works on both date and travel time.

![Choosing an office and date](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-06.png "Step 6 — pick an office by date and how easy it is to reach")

### 7. Choose a time

Green days on the calendar are available. Take the earliest slot of the day — you are less likely to be held up by the queue ahead of you.

![Choosing the date and time slot](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-07.png "Step 7 — green days are available; take the earliest slot")

### 8. State your reason

In `Motivo di richiesta appuntamento`, explain why you need the appointment, for example:

> Richiesta di prima attribuzione del codice fiscale per motivi di studio / soggiorno in Italia.
>
> (First application for an Italian tax code for study / residence in Italy.)

![Filling in the reason and contact details](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/booking-08.png "Step 8 — state the reason for the appointment and leave contact details")

Once submitted you get a confirmation email like this one. From there it is just document prep — turn up on time on the day.

![The booking confirmation email](/assets/posts/italy-codice-fiscale/feature-appointment-email.png "The confirmation email (appointment ID and ticket number redacted)")

## What to bring

- **Passport** (original)
- **Passport copy**
- **Copy of your visa page**
- **Photos**: two passport photos (mine were not needed, but bring them anyway)
- **Your address in Italy**: I used my dorm address
- **A pen**: something may need correcting on the spot
- **Form AA4/8**: pick it up at the office, or [print the form](https://www.agenziaentrate.gov.it/portale/documents/20143/278893/modello+editabile+AA4_8_AA4-8+ita.pdf/e614eda6-701e-c62f-76e5-bdfcd731060a) and fill it in beforehand

## On the day

I arrived early, and checking in at the front desk before my slot was fine. They hand you a number, you wait for it to be called, and that is it — the whole thing went smoothly.

A few things worth knowing at the counter:

- **Staff English is usually limited.** Have every document ready, or bring a translation device, so nothing stalls on communication.
- **Photograph the document they hand you** and keep the original safe. You will need it again for the residence permit, phone contract and transit pass.
- **Taiwanese applicants: check the code ends in `Z217`**, the country code for Taiwan. `Z220` is China — do not let them get mixed up.
